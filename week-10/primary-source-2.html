Immigration Act of 1924 (Johnson-Reed Act)
The annual quota of any nationality [shall be] two per centum of the number of foreign-born persons of such nationality resident in continental United States as determined by the United States census of 1890... The annual quota of any nationality for the fiscal year beginning July 1, 1927... shall be a number which bears the same ratio to 150,000 as the number of inhabitants in continental United States in 1920 having that national origin bears to the [total] number of inhabitants in continental United States in 1920, but the minimum quota of any nationality shall be 100.
Immigration Act of 1924 (Johnson-Reed Act), 68th Congress, excerpted and condensed
