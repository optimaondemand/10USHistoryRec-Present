🌐 Capstone Gallery: A Freedmen's Bureau Office

Beginning this week, you will build a personal gallery of artifacts across the quarter. It becomes the raw material for your Quarter 1 capstone project, so choose thoughtfully each week.

Join the shared VR space called the Q1 Capstone Gallery, set at a Freedmen's Bureau office in 1867. Examine the three objects on the office desk: a labor contract, a school primer, and a ration ticket. Place a sticky note on the item you find most historically significant, and explain why in two or three sentences. Then walk to your personal capstone shelf, marked with your name, and place a cloned copy of that object there with a signed sticky note. It is the first artifact in your gallery.

Submit a snapshot of your shelf and your sticky note text in Canvas. This activity takes about 15 to 20 minutes.
